## The gate that wasn't
Some teach that the "eye of the needle" was a small gate where a camel could squeeze through after being unloaded. DeWayne says there's no historical or archaeological support for that. Jesus meant impossible.

## Can I have money?
Yes. Abraham, Lot, Job, Lydia, and Zacchaeus were wealthy. Zacchaeus gave half away and repaid four times what he'd taken, but only after meeting Jesus.

## The rich young ruler
He'd kept the commands. Jesus asked him to sell everything and follow, and he walked away. The problem wasn't the money. It was what he couldn't let go of.

## The verse that ties it together
Mark 10:27: "With man this is impossible, but with God all things are possible." You can't get to heaven on your own anyway, and only with Christ can wealth not spoil who you are.

## Any idol
Money isn't sin. The love of money is. The same goes for the gym, or anything else that takes first place.

## From the group
One participant said what was missing was obedience and surrender. DeWayne agreed: the young man wanted one more box to check, when all he needed to do was accept a free gift.

## Try this
- Ask God to show you one thing that's taken first place, and give it back to Him.
