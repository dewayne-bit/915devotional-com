## The final piece isn't armor
The series wraps up with verse 18. DeWayne notes that prayer isn't listed as a piece of armor. It's the process that ties it together.

## A well-dressed soldier with no orders
You can have the best helmet, belt, shield, sword, and shoes, but if you're out of contact with the commander, you don't know where to go or when. What army equips you and then goes radio silent? If Paul wrote today, he might say "text in the Spirit on all occasions."

## All occasions, all kinds
All means all: mornings, meetings, meals, the car, good days and bad. Thanks, praise, and requests. And pray for all the Lord's people, including your pastor. He doesn't have a special hotline.

## Keep knocking
One participant read 1 Corinthians 4:7: everything we have was received. Another said to keep knocking, and to knock boldly. DeWayne suggested many of us learned as kids to stop asking because adults got tired of hearing "Dad, Dad, Dad." Our heavenly Father never does.

## Try this
- Set three reminders today to send God a quick prayer.
- Pray for your pastor by name this week.
