## Backwards
We decide on the trip, the job, or the business, get started, and then ask God to bless it. Proverbs 16:3 says to commit it to the Lord first.

## Weighing the heart
Verse 2 says God weighs motives. Two people can sell the same product the same way with the same result, one for God's glory and one for their own. God sees the difference.

## Two ways it goes wrong
DeWayne admits to the first one: giving it to God, deciding He's too slow, and grabbing the wheel back. The second is giving it to God and doing nothing, like sending no résumés because "God's got this." That's faith without works.

## The balance
Trust God as if it all depends on Him, and work as if it all depends on you. The effort is yours, and the result is His. Sometimes what looks like failure is God pointing you to the thing He had for you all along.

## Roll it
The Hebrew word for "commit" means to roll a heavy load off yourself and onto Him. As DeWayne put it, He's way stronger than you are.

## The tandem bike
A participant shared a church illustration: you and God on a tandem bike. When you steer, it's a struggle. When God steers, you still pedal, but the ride gets easier.

## Try this
- Before starting anything new this week, pray over it first.
