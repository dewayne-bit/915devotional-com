## Angels are real
Hebrews mentions angels often, and Abraham and Lot entertained them. But DeWayne says the point isn't to be nice in case someone is an angel. That turns hospitality into a lottery ticket.

## You never know
The real principle is that you never know whom you're serving or what God is doing through an open door. Jesus said whatever you do for the least of these, you do for Him (Matthew 25:40).

## Don't stop
"Do not forget" was written to believers who were already doing this. It's encouragement to keep going.

## What it looks like now
- The person in the back row at church, or the couple no one is talking to at your group. Go say hi.
- Table leaders checking in on new men during the week.
- The new hire's first week.
- The neighbor five houses down you've waved at for three years. Stop and meet them.

## A confirming moment
One participant shared that the topic lined up with something deeply personal in his own life that same day, and thanked the group for the daily call. DeWayne said they hadn't planned it. It was the Holy Spirit.

## Try this
- Introduce yourself to one person this week you'd normally just wave at.
