## Not about right or wrong
DeWayne opened by saying that praying this prayer daily is wonderful. His point was to show what Jesus was teaching with it.

## Two warnings first
Don't pray to be seen, though praying over your meal in a restaurant is fine when it's to honor God. And don't babble. You don't need big theological words. A sentence or two from the heart is a prayer.

## Line by line
- "Our Father in heaven": relationship, not religion.
- "Hallowed be your name": He is set apart and worshiped.
- "Your will be done": the hardest part, the same surrender Jesus prayed in Gethsemane.
- "Our daily bread": dependence, like manna that spoiled if kept for tomorrow.
- "Forgive us as we forgive": grace received and grace released. DeWayne referenced The Shack, where the hardest forgiveness is still required.
- "Deliver us from evil": protection we can't provide ourselves.

## Make it yours
A participant said the power comes from thinking about what each line means as you pray it. DeWayne said that was better than what he'd said.

## Try this
- Pray through the six lines tomorrow morning, one sentence each, in your own words.
