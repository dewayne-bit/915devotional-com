## What happened to your joy?
Paul wrote Galatians to people who had slipped back from grace into law-keeping. In 4:15 he asks them where their blessedness went. Grace and joy go together, and neither can be manufactured.

## The real question
Happiness depends on what's happening. Joy depends on whom you trust. That's how Paul and Silas could sing in a prison cell at midnight. DeWayne also points to James: count it all joy when you face trials, and expect more of them after you follow Christ, because now the enemy cares about you.

## Outside God's will
Someone asked what happens to joy when we go against God's will. DeWayne's answer: it leaves, because you've stopped trusting Him. Paul never wanted to be in prison, but he stayed in God's will there, so he kept his joy.

## Fruit is for others
An apple tree doesn't eat its own apples. The joy God grows in you is meant for the people around you.

## Try this
- When your mood drops today, ask yourself who you're trusting right now.
- Read Acts 16:22-34 and notice when the singing happens.
