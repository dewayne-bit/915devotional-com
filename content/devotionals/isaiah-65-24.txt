## Wrapping up prayer week
After the armor series ended with prayer, DeWayne finished the week on two Old Testament verses.

## Before they call
"Before they call I will answer." Israel was back in a good place with God, and He was responding the way a loving parent does. DeWayne told a story about his youngest daughter wiping out on her RipStik while camping. Before she could cry, he was already there.

## Near to all who call in truth
Psalm 145, an acrostic, says the Lord is near, not four universes away. He's near to all, not just the ordained. And He's near to those who call in truth. He isn't a gumball machine where you put in a quarter and get what you want.

## So why pray?
Because He wants to talk with us. DeWayne knows his daughter loves him, and he still gets excited when she calls.

## From the group
Someone repeated a pastor's line: we have microwave prayers and a Crock-Pot God. Another said the more you pray, the more your heart lines up with His will. A dad said he wants his kids to share their hearts with him, and God wants the same.

## Try this
- Spend five minutes today just talking to God, with no list.
