## Recorded at home
Another one DeWayne re-recorded after forgetting to hit record on Thursday. It closes the week's series on forgiveness with the hardest group to forgive: people in your own church.

## What to put on
Paul gives the church a wardrobe: compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, patience. Then he gets practical. Bear with each other. A church is wealthy and poor, every background, every personality, and you won't click with everyone. Bearing with someone means sticking with them anyway.

## Same process, different goal
With everyone, the process is the same: always forgive, as the Lord forgave you. The goal differs. With an unbeliever, you're showing them Jesus. With a believer, you're after restoration, because they're family. Wisdom still applies. You can forgive and keep someone at arm's length until they grow.

## Try this
- Read Colossians 3:12-17 and pick one quality to put on today.
- Think of a church relationship that's strained and take one step toward restoring it.
