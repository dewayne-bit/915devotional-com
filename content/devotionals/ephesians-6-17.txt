## Received, not grabbed
In Greek, Paul's "take" means to receive a gift. God hands us the helmet of salvation.

## The helmet keeps you in the fight
A soldier could survive a hit to the arm or leg, but not to the head. The enemy aims at the mind: you're not good enough, God isn't real. Paul says to take every thought captive (2 Corinthians 10:5) and points us to what to think about in Philippians 4:8.

## The short sword
This is the close-combat sword, for defending and attacking. DeWayne distinguishes logos, the whole Word, from rhema, a specific word spoken. Owning seven Bibles doesn't help if you can't quote three verses. Jesus answered temptation with specific verses from the Torah.

## Not an excuse
DeWayne admits he couldn't quote Scripture ten years ago. He learned because he saw why it mattered. A seeker standing in front of you needs your story and a verse, not a thick book handed over.

## From the group
One participant quoted Hosea 4:6: "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge." DeWayne added that knowledge is knowing there's a better lane, and wisdom is knowing how and when to get there.

## Try this
- Write one verse on an index card and read it at every meal this week.
- Next week, add another.
