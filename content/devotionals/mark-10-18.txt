## A question from Bible study
Someone asked DeWayne why Jesus said, "Why do you call me good? No one is good except God alone." It sounds like Jesus is saying He isn't good.

## The rich young ruler
The story is in Mark and Luke. Luke, who interviewed eyewitnesses, notes the man was a ruler, so we combine the accounts. Unlike the Pharisees, this man wasn't setting a trap. He ran up and knelt in public. He was sincere and driven, the top student who had kept every commandment.

## "Good" belonged to God
In that day you didn't call a rabbi "good." That word was for God. So Jesus wasn't denying He was good, or that He was God. He was asking the man, "Do you know what you're saying?"

## An inheritance
The man wanted one more task. Eternal life isn't earned. It's an inheritance that comes through relationship with the Giver. He walked away sad. Did he come back later? Scripture doesn't say.

## From the group
A participant stressed that Jesus wasn't telling everyone to give away everything. DeWayne agreed: wealth isn't a sin. They talked about tithing as giving first, before you know the outcome. As DeWayne said, it's all God's anyway. What He wants is our obedience and faith.
