## For a drifting church
James wrote to believers who were quarreling and flirting with the world. In 4:7-8 he lays out the way home in three steps. DeWayne joked it's clearer than IKEA directions.

## Submit
Submitting to God isn't weakness. It's getting into formation under the One in charge.

## Resist
From under His authority you can stand your ground, and the devil will flee. Not stall or think it over, but run.

## Draw near
"Come near to God and He will come near to you." God isn't waiting with folded arms. Most of us didn't fall away overnight. We drifted a little at a time, so coming back can start with a single step.

## What about discipline?
A participant asked how God's correction fits if He never forces us. DeWayne said the Spirit convicts, but the choice is still ours. Consequences come from love, the way a parent disciplines a child so they'll come home. Someone else compared it to touching a hot stove. You don't blame the stove, but you learn.

## Try this
- Before you open your inbox, name one thing that has been pulling you away.
- Take one specific step toward God today.
