## Stand firm
The armor series continues with verse 14: stand firm with the belt of truth and the breastplate of righteousness.

## The belt holds it all
For a Roman soldier, the belt held up the tunic and carried the sword and gear. Everything hung on it. As a builder, DeWayne calls truth the foundation: if it's crooked, everything above it is too. God's Word is truth, and the enemy's weapon is the lie. You can't resist a lie you don't recognize, so you have to know the Word, not just hear about it on Sunday.

## The breastplate guards the heart
The enemy's lies aim straight at the heart: the drugs, the people you hurt, the failures as a parent or spouse. The breastplate is Christ's righteousness, not ours. Unconfessed sin doesn't cancel salvation, but it leaves gaps, so confess and keep the breastplate in place.

## They flee
One participant tied it to James 4:7. Wearing the armor and resisting the devil makes him flee. Another was struck that the belt, the piece that holds everything together, is the one Paul calls truth.

## Try this
- Decide when you'll read Scripture each day this week, whether early, at lunch, or before bed.
- Answer one accusing thought with a verse about who you are in Christ.
