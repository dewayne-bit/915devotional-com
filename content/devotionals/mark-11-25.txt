## No exceptions
Jesus' wording in Mark 11:25 is sweeping: if you hold anything against anyone, forgive. That covers believers and unbelievers, family, coworkers, bosses, and the barista who forgot the whipped cream.

## Three points
First, it works both ways. If you're the one who did the wrong, asking forgiveness frees your heart just as much. Second, this isn't about losing salvation. Forgiveness from Christ was settled at the cross. Holding grudges stunts your growth and your prayer life. Third, forgiveness is not trust. You can forgive completely and still decide someone shouldn't have access to your life.

## Where's the line?
One participant asked how many chances to give someone who keeps breaking agreed boundaries. DeWayne's answer: always forgive, but reconciliation is a separate question. Pray for discernment, and if needed, love from a distance. Another participant added that a relationship that keeps pulling you to dim your light is a sign something has to change.

## Try this
- Before you pray today, ask if you're holding anything against anyone.
- If you owe someone an apology, make the call.
