## The question everybody asks
"I just want to know God's will for my life." DeWayne points to Romans 8:29, to be conformed to the image of His Son, and then to these three short verses Paul wrote to a church under persecution.

## Rejoice always
Joy isn't happiness. Happiness is an emotion. Joy is a habit built by knowing who you are in Christ, in seasons of "rainbows and Skittles" and in hard ones.

## Pray continually
This doesn't mean nonstop words from 8 a.m. to bedtime. It means living in God's presence, at work, at lunch, in the car, doing laundry.

## In, not for
Paul says give thanks in all circumstances, not for them. You don't thank God for cancer. You thank Him that He hasn't left you and is still at work.

## From the group
One participant uses an exercise DeWayne once taught. With a flat tire, thank God for the spare and the phone to call for help. Another said the enemy loves it when we complain. Another described how his heart changed in traffic, from cursing to praying. One man visited a ministry to the homeless and came away far more thankful.

## Try this
- Next time something goes wrong today, find one thing in it to thank God for.
