## The setup
After Stephen's death scattered the church, the gospel spread to Antioch, a city of Jews and Gentiles, Greek and Aramaic speakers, with almost nothing in common. A revival broke out there. Barnabas went to Tarsus to find Saul, brought him back, and the two taught there for a full year.

## Get a friend
Barnabas could have gone alone. He went looking for a partner. DeWayne shared something he heard from a guest speaker years ago: everyone needs someone less mature in the faith to pour into, and someone more mature to learn from. For him, finding younger believers to help was easy. Finding a mentor took much longer, and when he finally asked, it changed his walk.

## How to find one
Someone asked what to look for. DeWayne said to start by praying. Look for someone farther along, not necessarily older in the faith, ideally in a similar season of life, and probably in a men's or women's group at church. Then ask. As he tells his sales team, you get 100 percent of what you don't ask for.

## Christ ones
In Antioch, the only thing these people shared was Christ, so outsiders called them "Christians."

## Try this
- Pray for a mentor this week, then ask someone.
- Look for one newer believer you can encourage.
