## Never stop
The verbs in Luke 11:9-10 describe continuing action: keep asking, keep seeking, keep knocking. They follow a parable about a man banging on a friend's door at midnight until he gets help.

## Three steps
Ask: come humbly and say what you need. Seek: take action, because without it, it's wishful thinking. Knock: when you reach the door, don't stop. And "everyone who asks receives," though what you receive may be different and better than what you had in mind.

## With wisdom
Persistence needs discernment. You can keep "knocking" on a coworker about church until you do more harm than good. Pray and let the Holy Spirit set the pace.

## Do I need church?
A participant asked whether skipping church makes someone less of a Christian. DeWayne didn't judge anyone, but pointed to Scripture's call not to give up meeting together. Others weighed in. The church is people, not a building. Online and home groups count. And one man noted that church is where God puts people who rub you the wrong way so your character grows.

## Try this
- Pick one request you've stopped praying and start asking again.
- Ask what action you can take toward it this week.
