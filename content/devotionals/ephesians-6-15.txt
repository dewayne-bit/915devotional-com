## Boots on the ground
Continuing the armor series, DeWayne points out that soldiers often say the most important gear isn't the weapon. It's the boots. If you can't stand, nothing else you carry matters.

## Fitted for the fight
Roman soldiers wore thick sandals with spikes through the soles for grip, much like football cleats. They could hold their ground or move quickly.

## Ready and stable
Paul's word "readiness" means being prepared, since nobody stops in the middle of a battle to lace up, and it means being stable. The gospel gives both.

## A different kind of peace
Human peace is knowing your kid made it home last night. God's peace, as Philippians 4:7 says, surpasses all understanding. It rests on the simple message that Christ died for our sins.

## Paul in chains
One participant asked how dangerous Paul must have seemed to be chained to a guard. DeWayne noted some scholars think the chain let him move around under watch, while others picture a dungeon. Another pointed out the threat was to the rulers' power, not public safety.

## Try this
- Read Philippians 4:4-9 alongside this verse.
- Before a tough conversation today, ask God for His peace first.
