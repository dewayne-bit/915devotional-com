## A test from his daughter
DeWayne's youngest was about to leave for college. She sat him down for advice and then called him out: "Dad, you haven't brought up any Scripture." So he gave her 1 Peter 3:15, and brought it to the call the next morning.

## Always prepared
Peter wrote to believers being slandered for their faith. Always be prepared to give an answer, the Greek word apologia, a defense, for the hope you have. Preparation is work you do today for a conversation you may have tomorrow. DeWayne compares it to a sales script: there's a reason you know what you'll say before you say it.

## Someone has to ask
Notice that Peter assumes someone asks. That only happens when your life looks different from theirs. And when they ask, they want a short, honest answer, not a 45-minute sermon.

## Gentleness and respect
Gentleness is strength under control. "Turn or burn" may be technically true, but it doesn't win people.

## Try this
- Write your testimony in two or three sentences tonight.
- Practice saying it out loud in under thirty seconds.
