## A busy Sunday
DeWayne's church had a lot of baptisms, then a Friendsgiving for a new campus. Asked to pray over the meal, he started thinking about confession.

## Confess what?
Proverbs 28:13 is about sin, and that matters. But baptism is also a confession, a public statement of an inward faith. DeWayne wondered whether thanksgiving should be one too.

## Recognition isn't confession
Knowing you did wrong and keeping it to yourself isn't confession. The demons recognized who Jesus was but never called Him Lord.

## The dishes
His wife came home after a long weekend to find the dishes done. Hours later she noticed and said, "Babe, you did the dishes? Thank you." She went beyond noticing to saying it, and that meant something.

## Thankful for the layoff
Philippians 4:6 says to pray with thanksgiving before the answer comes. Imagine telling your group you just got fired, but you're thankful because God has something better. Someone in the room might be hiring.

## From the group
A participant read Philippians 4:8. DeWayne said the "positive mental attitude" of business books came from Scripture first.

## Try this
- Thank one person out loud today for something you noticed.
