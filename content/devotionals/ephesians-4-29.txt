## Two passages, one morning
DeWayne reads the Proverbs chapter that matches the day of the month. On the 12th, Proverbs 12:14 lined up with the Ephesians verse he'd planned.

## More than four-letter words
Unwholesome talk includes cussing, but also gossip, sarcasm that serves only us, constant criticism, and jokes that hurt. As he put it, half of a joke is usually true.

## Never neutral
Every word builds up or tears down. Paul says to speak "only" what helps, according to the other person's needs. Saying nothing when correction is needed doesn't help either. Pray for the wisdom to know when and how.

## Who's listening
Paul adds "that it may benefit those who listen." Other people overhear what we say.

## The fruit of your lips
Proverbs 12:14 says good words fill the speaker with good things, and God rewards the work of their hands. Constant negativity doesn't attract good.

## From the group
One participant said the morning call renews her mind. DeWayne agreed: we don't live in the Matrix, so the Word gets in through daily repetition.

## Try this
- For one day, notice every word you say about someone who isn't in the room.
