## After a Sunday altar call
The day before, DeWayne watched a lot of people go forward for prayer at church. That stirred up Jesus' words in the Sermon on the Mount: don't pray like the hypocrites who perform on street corners. Go into your room, shut the door, and pray to your Father in secret.

## What Jesus was correcting
The religious leaders prayed to be admired, and that admiration was all the reward they got. Jesus isn't banning group prayer, praying with your spouse, or a quick prayer in the car with your eyes open. He's going after motive.

## An honest admission
DeWayne said most of his praying happens with his wife or on the road, and he rarely shuts himself away just with God. Busyness can hide a lack of real intimacy. One participant described the closeness that only comes in that private place, and the group remembered the movie War Room, where a woman clears out a closet to pray.

## Try this
- Pick a spot, even a closet, and spend ten undistracted minutes with God this week.
- Leave time to be still and listen, not just talk.
